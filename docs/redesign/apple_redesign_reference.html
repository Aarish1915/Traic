Disciplines

Innovation in every line of code.

Six engineering verticals collaborating under one lab roof to build production hardware.

Autonomous Robotics

SLAM navigation, custom drive trains, and competition platforms built for high reliability.

Embedded Systems

Bare-metal C/C++, ARM Cortex microcontrollers, RTOS kernels, and high-speed communication buses.

Connected IoT

Sensor telemetry meshes, MQTT broker setups, and smart campus environmental monitoring.

Applied Edge AI

Embedded computer vision pipelines, ONNX runtimes, and real-time obstacle identification.

Unmanned Aerial Systems

Custom quadcopter frames, flight controller firmware calibration, and autonomous waypoints.
